Getting Started

At the sixth hour of an endless eclipse, the great solar observatory has gone dark. You are the last Lightkeeper. Relight its chambers and restart the solar engine before the darkness becomes permanent.

What you need

  • A desktop or laptop browser: Chrome, Edge or Firefox.
  • A keyboard. A mouse is optional (menus can be clicked).
  • Speakers or headphones: the soundscape tells you a lot.
  • About 10–15 minutes for the six campaign chambers.

Starting a game

  1. Open adil-infosec.github.io/SIX-SECONDS-OF-SUN.
  2. On the title screen, choose PLAY. It continues from the next chamber you have not cleared yet.
  3. LEVEL SELECT lets you replay any unlocked chamber; HOW TO PLAY shows a one-screen summary.
SOUND Browsers only allow sound after your first key press or click, so the music begins as soon as you touch the game.

Fire, Shadow and Time

Every chamber is a sealed room with an exit gate. Open the gate, step into the portal behind it, and the chamber is cleared. Three ideas open every gate, and they work as one chain.

FIRE

Stand next to a brazier and press E. The main beacon floods the room with light. No fire, no shadows.

SHADOW

Your body blocks the light and throws a shadow straight away from the flame. Put that shadow on a rune to power it.

TIME

Press Q and your last six seconds of movement become a looping cyan echo. Its shadow powers runes too.

Casting a shadow

  • Your shadow always points away from the lit beacon. To aim it, stand between the flame and the target.
  • It starts at your feet and widens with distance. Stepping toward or away from the flame slides it along the floor.
  • Hold the shadow on a rune: its ring fills in under half a second, then the rune lights up (SHADOW LOCK).

Your echo (Q)

  • The brace on your wrist remembers your last 6 seconds. The MEMORY ring at the top of the screen fills as you move; with at least 1 second stored it glows cyan and Q is ready.
  • The echo replays that path forever. To make it stand still, stand still for six seconds before you press Q.
  • Its shadow works exactly like yours, and standing in your echo's shadow protects you from fire.
  • Pressing Q again replaces the old echo. The echo cannot light braziers or leave the room.

Runes and gates

Latching runeSolid ring. Once powered it stays powered.
Continuous runeDashed outer ring. Only powered while a shadow is on it: hold it yourself, or leave your echo there.
The gateIcons beside it show what it needs (flames, relays, runes). It opens when all are met and closes again if one is lost, unless you are standing in the doorway.

The Screen, Hazards & Stars

Reading the screen

Top leftChamber number and name.
Top centreMEMORY ring around the Q key and seconds stored; the bar beside it shows where your echo is in its six-second loop.
Top rightHearts, the chamber clock (it starts when you first move) and the pause sign.
Bottom leftGoal badges: FLAME lit, RUNE powered, GATE locked / open. They light up as you complete them.
In the roomAn E key appears over a brazier you can light; short hints mark what to try next.

Fire scanners

  • Burning bands sweep back and forth across striped hazard lanes.
  • A dashed line shows where the band will be a moment later, and a red ring flashes around you just before it would touch you.
  • Cross when the band is far away, or stand inside your echo's shadow: the fire cannot burn you there.

Hearts and checkpoints

  • You have 3 hearts. A burn costs one, and you reappear at the last checkpoint you touched (or the start), protected for one second.
  • Lose all three and the chamber is EXTINGUISHED: it restarts by itself. Press R any time to restart sooner.

Stars

RatingHow to earn it
★★★Clear within the chamber's par time with no hits.
★★Clear within 1.5 × par with at most one hit.
★Any clear.

Your best time and best stars are kept for every chamber; beating either shows NEW BEST.

Menus, Saving & Help

Chamber list

  • Cards show each chamber's number, best stars and best time. Locked chambers are dimmed.
  • Two pages: CAMPAIGN and BONUS. Switch with Q / E, or click the tabs.
  • The arrow at top left returns to the title; the speaker toggles sound.

Pause & settings

  • Esc pauses: RESUME, RESTART or back to CHAMBERS.
  • The game also pauses itself if the window loses focus, so nothing can hurt you while you are away.
  • The gear on the title screen opens Settings: sound and screen shake.

Saving

  • Progress saves automatically in your browser after every clear: unlocked chambers, best times, stars and settings.
  • It is stored on this computer, in this browser. Clearing the browser's site data resets it.

Troubleshooting

No soundPress any key or click the game once; check M and the speaker icon; make sure the browser tab is not muted.
Blank or black screenThe game switches to its Canvas renderer automatically if WebGL fails. You can force it by adding ?renderer=canvas to the address.
Choppy movementClose other heavy tabs, and turn on hardware acceleration in the browser settings.
Game paused by itselfThe window lost focus. Click the game and press Esc or choose RESUME.
Stuck in a puzzlePress R to restart instantly; re-read the hint in the room; check the goal badges at bottom left.

Quick questions

Why is Q not working? The echo unlocks in chamber 3, needs at least one second of memory, and has a short cooldown after each use.

Why won't the brazier light? Stand right next to it, until the E key appears above it. A prism takes light only after the main beacon is lit.

My shadow is on the rune but nothing happens. Hold it there a little longer, and check that the beacon is lit: no fire, no shadow.
