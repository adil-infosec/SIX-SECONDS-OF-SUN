2Story & World

Premise. At the edge of a dying world, an endless eclipse has frozen the great solar observatory. You are the last Lightkeeper, bound to a clockwork brace that captures and replays the movement of the past.

Objective. Cross six ancient chambers, reignite their solar beacons, and restart the clockwork solar engine before the darkness becomes permanent.

I · Kindling1–2Rekindle the first lens; learn that darkness itself awakens hidden runes.
II · The Brace3–5Summon time-echoes, hold doors from two places at once, survive sweeping fire.
III · Dawn6Synchronise the sun core and its relays, and lift the eclipse at sunrise.

3Core Theme Synergy: Fire → Shadow → Time

The three competition themes are not separate features; they are one physical chain.

FIRE

Lighting a beacon floods the chamber with light. Without fire, no shadows exist.

→

SHADOW

The Lightkeeper's body blocks the light and projects a directional shadow. Hold it over a dark rune and the rune powers a gate.

→

TIME

Press Q: your last six seconds become a looping cyan echo that casts its own shadow, holding a rune or sheltering you from fire while you act elsewhere.

Light makes shadow; time multiplies it. Every chamber recombines the three in a new configuration.

5Judge Highlights

  • Authored campaign. Six handcrafted chambers with rising puzzle density, then six bonus chambers with new mechanics: twin seals, moving pillars, a rotating fire lamp, sequence runes, a prism relay and an echo-guarded gauntlet. No procedural filler.
  • Visual & audio polish. Dark observatory aesthetic in neon cyan against amber, an animated Lightkeeper and echo, particle feedback on every key action, and a fully procedural WebAudio soundscape.
  • Stability & quality. Deterministic shadow and room simulation; 220 automated tests, including playthroughs proving every chamber solvable; frame-rate-independent timing; validated local saves; automatic Canvas fallback if WebGL fails.
